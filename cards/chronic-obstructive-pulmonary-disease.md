# Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease — Adult (ICD-10 J44.9)

*As of 2026-10-12. DRAFT — not clinically reviewed. Not for clinical use.*

## Quick Summary
Progressive, largely irreversible airflow limitation from chronic airway/alveolar inflammation; diagnosis requires postbronchodilator FEV1/FVC below 0.70. Overwhelmingly smoking-related, rising sharply with age (US age-adjusted prevalence 3.8% of adults 18+, 10.5% at 75+); 5th-leading US cause of death in 2023 (141,733 deaths). Presents as chronic dyspnea, cough, and sputum production punctuated by exacerbations. GOLD 2026 reframes management around **"disease activity"** (ongoing inflammation, symptom variability, exacerbation risk) layered on ABE risk groups, pairing stepped inhaled bronchodilator +/- ICS therapy with smoking cessation, vaccination, and pulmonary rehabilitation.

## Quick News Update
- **2025-12-08** — GOLD 2026 Report (v1.3; pocket guide v1.1, 2025-11-20) released, superseding GOLD 2024/2025: reframes COPD around disease activity; **escalate therapy after even one moderate exacerbation** (prior reports used 2+/yr); RSV vaccination now recommended for adults 50 and over; GOLD does **not** recommend population-wide spirometry screening. Publication date is ambiguous across sources (one gives 2025-11-10). [GOLD 2026 report](https://goldcopd.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/GOLD-REPORT-2026-v1.3-8Dec2025_WMV2.pdf)
- **2024-09** (outside the usual 18-month window; carried as the only approved COPD biologic) — FDA approved **Dupixent (dupilumab)**, first biologic for COPD, add-on for an eosinophilic phenotype (blood eosinophils ≥300/µL in pivotal trials), on BOREAS/NOTUS (30-34% exacerbation-rate reduction). [label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/761055s064lbl.pdf)
- **2024-06-26** (outside the window; carried as the newest inhaled mechanism) — FDA approved **Ohtuvayre (ensifentrine)**, first new inhaled COPD mechanism in over 20 years, on ENHANCE-1/ENHANCE-2. [FDA approval](https://www.fda.gov/media/182289/download)
- Context: COPD age-adjusted prevalence 3.8% of US adults 18+, rising to 10.5% at 75+; data year 2023 (published May 2025). [CDC NCHS Data Brief 529](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/databriefs/db529.pdf)
