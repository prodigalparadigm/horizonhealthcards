# Hypertension — Adult, non-pregnant (ICD-10 I10)

## Quick Summary
Persistently elevated blood pressure; a leading modifiable risk factor for stroke, MI, heart failure, and CKD. Affects an estimated 47.7% of US adults (CDC). Diagnosed by repeated office/ambulatory BP measurement and staged by severity. Per the 2025 AHA/ACC/multisociety guideline, management is lifestyle modification plus pharmacotherapy from three first-line classes (thiazide/thiazide-like diuretic, long-acting dihydropyridine CCB, ACE inhibitor or ARB), targeting <130/80 for most adults.

## Quick News Update
- **2025-05 (online) / 2025-08 (press)** — 2025 AHA/ACC/multisociety High Blood Pressure Guideline (JACC) — first major revision since 2017: universal **<130/80** target for most adults; **PREVENT equations replace Pooled Cohort Equations** for 10-yr CVD risk; renal denervation introduced as adjunctive option for selected resistant hypertension; primary aldosteronism screening expanded regardless of serum potassium. [JACC](https://www.jacc.org/doi/10.1016/j.jacc.2025.05.007)
- **2026-05-18** — FDA approved **baxdrostat (Baxfendy, AstraZeneca)**, first-in-class aldosterone synthase inhibitor, as add-on for uncontrolled hypertension. Phase 3 BaxHTN: 2 mg reduced seated SBP 15.7 mmHg from baseline, 9.8 mmHg vs placebo at week 12. See *Not on this card* — no drug row pending label verification. [label (unverified)](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/219878Orig1s000lbl.pdf)
- Context: only **20.7%** of US adults with hypertension are controlled to <130/80. [CDC NCHS Data Brief 511](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/databriefs/db511.pdf)
