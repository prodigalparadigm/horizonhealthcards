# Chronic Kidney Disease — Adult, Non-Dialysis (N18.9)

*As of 2026-10-12. Draft — pending factchecker review.*

## Quick Summary

Progressive loss of kidney function/structure for >3 months; staged by KDIGO 2024 eGFR
category (G1 90+ down to G5 <15, incl. G3a 45–59, G3b 30–44) crossed with albuminuria category
(A1 <30, A2 30–300, A3 >300 mg/g ACR) — the KDIGO "heat map." Diabetes and hypertension are the
leading causes. ~35.5M US adults affected (~14%, >1 in 7), ~9 in 10 unaware; 34% prevalence at
age 65+. Managed with kidney-protective therapy — RAAS blockade (ACEi/ARB) as the BP/proteinuria
foundation, SGLT2i for CKD with proteinuria (with or without diabetes), finerenone added on top
in T2D-CKD, semaglutide in T2D-CKD, statins for CV risk — plus complication management (anemia,
CKD-MBD, metabolic acidosis if serum bicarbonate ≤18 mmol/L, hyperkalemia).

## Quick News Update

- **2025-01-28** — FDA approved semaglutide (Ozempic) to reduce the risk of kidney disease
  worsening, kidney failure, and CV death in adults with T2D and CKD — first GLP-1 RA with this
  indication (FLOW trial: 24% relative, 4.9% absolute risk reduction at 3 years). [sponsor release](https://www.ozempic.com/content/dam/diabetes-patient/ozempic/pdfs/Ozempic_CKD_sNDA_Press_Release_January_28_2025.pdf)
- **2024-03** — KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline for CKD, first full update since 2012:
  cystatin C–based eGFR emphasized for risk stratification, individualized 2–5 year kidney-failure
  risk prediction, updated drug stewardship. [KDIGO](https://kdigo.org/kdigo-announces-publication-of-2024-ckd-guideline/)

## Not on this card

- Daprodustat (Jesduvroq) gets no row — HIF-PHI approved for anemia of CKD in
  **dialysis-dependent patients only**; not approved for this non-dialysis population.
- Sodium bicarbonate gets no row — no FDA label for CKD metabolic acidosis; guideline-driven
  off-label use (KDIGO bicarbonate threshold named in summary instead).
- KDIGO's numeric BP and albuminuria targets were not pulled from the primary PDF — a real gap
  on a CKD card.
- Semaglutide's boxed-warning wording and eGFR floor for the CKD indication are unverified
  (source PDF not machine-readable); ACEi/ARB fetal-toxicity wording was rendered across label
  families rather than parsed from one document. All need fact-check.
- Finerenone: two NDA numbers (215341, 217188) appeared with different label years; eGFR dosing
  above is from the 2022 215341 label and may not be current.
- "Four pillars" is a literature convention, not KDIGO's own term.
- CKD-MBD specifics (phosphate, PTH, vitamin D targets) not gathered.
