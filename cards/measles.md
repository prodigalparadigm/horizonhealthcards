# Measles (Rubeola) — All Ages (B05.9)

*As of 2026-10-12. Draft — pending factchecker review.*

## Quick Summary

Airborne and among the most contagious viral diseases known — secondary attack rate ≥90% in
susceptible household/institutional contacts. Prodrome of fever (up to 105°F), malaise, cough,
coryza, conjunctivitis, then pathognomonic Koplik spots, then maculopapular rash (~14 days
post-exposure). 20% of US cases are hospitalized; ~1 encephalitis case and 2–3 deaths per 1,000
reported cases. **No FDA-approved antiviral exists — management is supportive care only.**
Vitamin A is a supportive adjunct, not a treatment or vaccine substitute. Contagious 4 days
before to 4 days after rash onset; isolate 4 days post-rash if immunocompetent, for duration of
illness if immunocompromised (prolonged shedding). (CDC Clinical Overview of Measles)

## Quick News Update

- **2026, as of 2026-10-08** — 4,080 confirmed US cases, 42 outbreaks, 96% outbreak-associated
  year-to-date — the worst year since the early 1990s. [CDC data](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html)
- **2025 (full year)** — 2,289 confirmed US cases, 48 outbreaks, 90% outbreak-associated. [CDC data](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/data-research/index.html)
- **2025** — CDC Health Alert Network notice on the expanding outbreak and traveler guidance. [CDC HAN00522](https://www.cdc.gov/han/php/notices/han00522.html)

## Current Drug Resources

No antiviral is listed below because none exists; no investigational antiviral candidate was
found in this research pass. Rows cover prevention (vaccine), post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP),
and supportive adjunct therapy only.

| Drug | Class | Line | Route | Key warning | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M-M-R II (Merck) | Live attenuated MMR vaccine | 1L — routine immunization / PEP ≤72h | SC or IM | No boxed warning; contraindicated: pregnancy, immunosuppression, anaphylaxis to neomycin, moderate/severe febrile illness; caution w/ history of febrile seizures or thrombocytopenia | [label](https://www.fda.gov/files/vaccines,%20blood%20&%20biologics/published/Package-Insert-Measles-Mumps-and-Rubella-Virus-Vaccine-Live_2.pdf) |
| ProQuad / MMRV (Merck) | Live attenuated MMR + varicella | 1L — routine immunization / PEP ≤72h | SC | No boxed warning; W&P 5.1: dose 1 in unvaccinated children 12–23mo linked to higher fever/febrile seizure rate (~1 extra per 2,300–2,600 doses) vs. separate MMR+varicella; caution w/ personal/family febrile seizure history | [label](https://www.fda.gov/media/147563/download) |
| GamaSTAN S/D (Grifols) | Human immune globulin | PEP — ≤6d, when MMR contraindicated | IM only — explicitly not for IV use | No boxed warning noted; 0.25 mL/kg routine or 0.5 mL/kg (max 15 mL) immunocompromised | [label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=561f48de-2697-4a35-8233-bd1cdb73438f&audience=professional) |
| IGIV (class — no single labeled product) | Human immune globulin | PEP — immunocompromised, ≤6d | IV | 100–400 mg/kg; CDC recommends at class level — no single product carries a dedicated measles-PEP label indication | [CDC PEP guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/downloads/measles-immune-globulin-post-exposure-prophylaxis.pdf) |
| Vitamin A (retinol) | Supportive nutritional adjunct — not an antiviral | Supportive — confirmed/severe/hospitalized cases | PO | No FDA label for this use (CDC/AAP guidance, not label-driven); dosing: <6mo 50,000 IU, 6–11mo 100,000 IU, ≥12mo 200,000 IU, one dose at diagnosis + repeat next day; overuse risk: hepatic, bone, CNS, skin toxicity; not a vaccine substitute | [CDC guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/measles/media/pdfs/2026/05/measles-prevention-and-treatment-overview.pdf) |

MMR is preferred PEP within 72h of exposure; immune globulin (≤6 days) is reserved for those who
cannot receive MMR — infants <6mo, pregnant people, severely immunocompromised, other MMR
contraindications. IG gives only ~3–4 weeks of protection and is not a vaccine substitute. (CDC)

## Not on this card

- MMRV-vs-separate-MMR scheduling under age 4 — the Sept 2025 ACIP vote against MMRV under age
  4 and the March 2026 court stay of that vote (*AAP v. Kennedy*) leave the net operative
  recommendation unsettled; the stay is Tier 4 only. Check the live CDC ACIP schedule page
  before advising on sequence. (ProQuad's label-level febrile-seizure warning above is Tier 1
  and stays on the card regardless.)
- Exact 2026 case count — CDC's case/outbreak page updates on a rolling basis; a count as-of
  2026-10-08 was observed to shift by a few cases between retrievals in one session. Re-pull
  before citing an exact figure.
- Pricing, reimbursement, J-codes — not researched.
- Whether US measles elimination status is at risk — no primary source located either way.
- Antiviral therapy for measles — none exists; this is not an omission for space, there is no
  approved or pipeline antiviral agent to list.
