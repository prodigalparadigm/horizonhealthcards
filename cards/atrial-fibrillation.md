# Atrial Fibrillation — Adult (I48.91)

*As of 2026-10-12. Draft — pending factchecker review.*

## Quick Summary

Most common sustained arrhythmia: disorganized atrial electrical activity causing an
irregular, often rapid ventricular rate. Risk rises with age, hypertension, heart failure,
valvular disease, obesity, OSA; presents as palpitations, fatigue, dyspnea, or is found
incidentally on ECG. US prevalence ~10.55M adults (4.48%) (2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS guideline).
Management runs two concurrent axes — stroke prevention (anticoagulate when estimated annual
stroke/thromboembolic risk is ≥2%, by CHA2DS2-VASc/ATRIA/GARFIELD-AF; DOAC preferred over
warfarin except mitral stenosis or mechanical valve) and rate- or rhythm-control (early
rhythm control favored for sinus maintenance; catheter ablation is 1L in selected symptomatic
paroxysmal AF) — plus risk-factor modification.

## Quick News Update

- **2026 (signal)** — Factor XI inhibitor **asundexian failed** in AF: phase 3 OCEANIC-AF
  stopped early for lack of efficacy — stroke/systemic embolism in 1.3% on asundexian vs.
  0.4% on apixaban. [trial report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13156108/)
- **As of 2026-10** — No factor XI inhibitor is FDA-approved for AF; milvexian's
  LIBREXIA-AF phase 3 program continues. [J&J press release](https://www.jnj.com/media-center/press-releases/milvexian-granted-u-s-fda-fast-track-designation-for-all-three-indications-under-evaluation-in-phase-3-librexia-program-ischemic-stroke-acute-coronary-syndrome-and-atrial-fibrillation)
- **2024-01-31** — FDA approved the **FARAPULSE Pulsed Field Ablation System** (first US PFA
  system) for pulmonary vein isolation in drug-refractory, symptomatic paroxysmal AF (ADVENT
  trial). [ACC coverage](https://www.acc.org/latest-in-cardiology/articles/2024/01/31/17/59/fda-update-agency-approves-farapulse-pulsed-field-ablation-system)
