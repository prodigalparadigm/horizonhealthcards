# Major Depressive Disorder — Adult (F32.9)

*As of 2026-10-12. Draft — pending factchecker review.*

## Quick Summary

Depressed mood/anhedonia plus sleep/appetite/concentration/energy/guilt/psychomotor/suicidality symptoms ≥2 weeks causing impairment; tracked via PHQ-9 (≥10 suggests MDD, 5-pt drop = response, <5 = remission).
USPSTF 2023 (Grade B): screen all adults 19+, incl. pregnant/postpartum; insufficient evidence (I statement) for routine suicide-risk screening generally.
**VA/DoD (2022)**, the current actionable US guideline: first-line SSRI/SNRI/bupropion/mirtazapine/trazodone, plus newly added vilazodone/vortioxetine, +/- CBT/IPT/newly added short-term psychodynamic therapy; trial ~4–8 wks; step 2 = augment (atypical antipsychotic, lithium, T3, 2nd antidepressant) or switch; esketamine/ketamine or rTMS after 2+ failed trials defines TRD.
**Guideline-currency flag:** APA's only full MDD CPG is still the 3rd edition (Oct 2010, reaffirmed 2015), archived as "Legacy" — no newer full APA CPG exists; this card anchors to VA/DoD 2022, not APA.

## Quick News Update

- **2015 (reaffirmed), re-confirmed 2026-10-12** — No new full APA MDD guideline exists; the 2010/2015 edition is still APA's only full CPG, now archived under "Legacy Practice Guidelines." [APA](https://www.psychiatry.org/psychiatrists/practice/clinical-practice-guidelines/legacy-practice-guidelines-for-major-depressive-di)
- **2025-03-24** — SPRAVATO REMS modified (most recent update); certified-setting dispensing/monitoring requirements continue. [FDA letter](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2025/211243Orig1s021ltr.pdf)
- **2025-01-21** — FDA approved Spravato (esketamine) as **monotherapy** for TRD; previously required a concomitant oral antidepressant. [FDA letter](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2025/211243Orig1s021ltr.pdf)
- **2025** — Auvelity approved for agitation in Alzheimer's dementia — separate from its MDD indication. [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-non-antipsychotic-drug-treat-agitation-associated-dementia)

## Not on this card

- Quetiapine XR (Seroquel XR), brexpiprazole (Rexulti) — cut for the 8-agent space limit (see footer); same boxed warning class as aripiprazole
- Zuranolone (Zurzuvae), brexanolone (Zulresso) — postpartum-depression only; zuranolone got an FDA Complete Response Letter for general MDD, so neither gets a row
- Brexanolone commercial discontinuation (reported 2025-01-01) — Tier 4 only, no FDA notice located
- Lumateperone (Caplyta) for MDD — unverified; only an APA practice-update snippet, no label pulled; omitted
- Trazodone, vilazodone, vortioxetine, lithium, T3 augmentation — named in VA/DoD 2022 but no FDA label pulled this pass
- Ketamine (non-esketamine), rTMS as TRD options — named in VA/DoD 2022 but no device/drug label pulled this pass
