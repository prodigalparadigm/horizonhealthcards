# Source hierarchy

Cite the highest tier you can reach. The tier is part of the claim: a society guideline
and a company press release must never look alike on the page.

## Tier 1 — Regulatory and guideline primary

Treat as authoritative. Always capture version/year and date.

- **FDA** — approval letters, labels, and review documents at `accessdata.fda.gov`;
  advisory committee materials; `fda.gov/drugs/.../novel-drug-approvals`
- **EMA** — EPARs and CHMP opinions, when EU status informs US timing
- **Society guidelines, on the society's own domain** — pick the one that governs the
  disease area:

| Area | Body |
|---|---|
| Oncology | NCCN, ASCO, ESMO |
| Cardiology | ACC/AHA, HFSA, ESC |
| Diabetes / endocrine | ADA, AACE |
| Gastroenterology | ACG, AGA |
| Pulmonary | ATS, GOLD, GINA |
| Neurology | AAN, AHS |
| Rheumatology | ACR, EULAR |
| Infectious disease | IDSA, CDC/ACIP |
| Nephrology | KDIGO |
| Psychiatry | APA |

- **CMS** — NCDs, LCDs, fee schedules, and J-code assignments for the economics pass

## Tier 2 — Trial and literature primary

- **ClinicalTrials.gov** — the record of truth for phase, status, enrollment, sites, and
  posted dates. Cite the NCT ID and note the record's last-update date.
- Peer-reviewed publications with a DOI
- Published conference abstracts (ASCO, ACC, ADA, DDW, AAN, ACR, ASH) — name the meeting
  and year; abstracts are preliminary, so say so

## Tier 3 — Company primary

Reliable for what a company is doing, self-interested about what it means.

- Investor relations pipeline pages — capture the as-of date; pipeline pages change
  silently, and an asset's quiet disappearance is itself a finding
- SEC filings: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K
- Earnings call transcripts
- Company press releases

## Tier 4 — Trade press

Endpoints News, Fierce Biotech/Pharma, STAT, Evaluate, BioPharma Dive.

**A lead, never a citation.** Chase every Tier 4 item to the filing, trial record, or
release and cite that instead. If it cannot be reached, mark the claim
`[UNVERIFIED — Tier 4 only]` and list it under Could not confirm.

## Traps

- **Stale guidelines.** Search results favor older, better-linked PDFs. Verify you have
  the current version on the society's own site before citing. Never cite without a year.
- **Press-release phase inflation.** "Pivotal" and "registrational" are marketing words.
  ClinicalTrials.gov sets the phase.
- **Secondary aggregators** of prevalence figures citing each other in a loop. Run the
  number to its origin — CDC, a registry, or a published epidemiology paper — or report
  the loop under Could not confirm.
- **Expanded access and compassionate use** read like approvals in company language.
  They are not.
- **Price.** List price (WAC) is not net price is not what the system pays. Say which one
  you have. Net prices are usually unknowable from public sources — that belongs in gaps.
