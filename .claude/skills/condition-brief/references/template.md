# Brief template

Follow this structure exactly: six sections, this order. Replace bracketed text. Delete
nothing — a section with no content says "No material findings; see Could not confirm."

---

```markdown
# <Condition segment> — Strategic Brief

**As of:** YYYY-MM-DD · **Scope:** <segment assumed> · **Frame:** US / FDA / Medicare &
commercial · **Sources:** N (Tier 1–2: N) · **Searches:** N

> Prepared for health system strategy. Not clinical decision support; not for the care of
> an individual patient.

## 1. Snapshot

Three to five sentences. What the condition is, the segment this brief covers and why
that segmentation, US burden with a source, and the one sentence on why this is on the
strategy radar right now.

| | |
|---|---|
| US prevalence / incidence | [figure](source) |
| Typical site of care today | ... |
| Annual cost per patient | [figure](source) |
| Pipeline temperature | Hot / Warming / Quiet — one clause of why |

## 2. Current Standard of Care

Name the governing guideline with version and year, linked. Then the algorithm by line of
therapy.

| Line | Therapy | Modality | Site of care | Guideline basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1L | ... | oral / infused / device / procedure | clinic / HOPD / inpatient | [(Society vX.YYYY)](url) |

End with **Where practice diverges from guideline** — two or three bullets, each sourced.
This is where a strategy team finds its opening.

## 3. What's Changing — Emerging Treatments

Phase 2 and later only. Earlier assets go in one summary line, not the table.

| Asset | Sponsor | Mechanism | Modality | Phase | Next catalyst | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | ... | Ph3 | PDUFA 2027-Q1 / readout 2026-H2 / not posted | [NCT########](url) |

Then **Recently failed or discontinued** — a table in the same shape. The graveyard tells
a strategy team which mechanisms to stop believing in, and it is the section competitors'
briefs omit.

## 4. Key Companies & Institutions

| Organization | Role | Why they matter here | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | pharma / device / academic center / network | one clause | [link](url) |

Then **Deals** — M&A, licensing, and partnership, with value and announcement date,
each sourced. Omit the subsection only if there are none, and say so.

## 5. Health System Implications

The section the brief exists for. No new facts here — only consequences of Sections 2–4,
each one tied back to the finding that drives it. Cover, in this order, dropping any that
genuinely do not apply:

- **Capability and infrastructure.** What the system must be able to do that it may not
  do today — apheresis, REMS certification, specialty pharmacy, imaging, genetic testing,
  infusion chairs, a procedure suite.
- **Site-of-care shift.** Which direction volume moves (inpatient → HOPD → clinic → home)
  and over what horizon. Name the revenue at stake, not just the direction.
- **Drug spend and reimbursement exposure.** Pharmacy vs medical benefit, 340B relevance,
  buy-and-bill risk, the agents that will show up in next year's budget.
- **Workforce.** Which specialties and support roles the new standard of care requires.
- **Market and referral dynamics.** Who in the region is building this, where leakage
  would go, who is an affiliation target.
- **Trial participation.** Open trials recruiting in or near the system's footprint and
  what sponsoring one would require.

Each bullet: the implication, the horizon (now / 12–24 mo / 3–5 yr), and the finding it
rests on.

## 6. Watch List & Open Questions

**Dated catalysts** — what to put on the calendar.

| Date | Event | Why it matters | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-Q4 | Phase 3 readout, <asset> | Would move 2L standard of care | [NCT########](url) |

**Could not confirm** — mandatory, never empty.

- Claims found only in secondary sources, with what was tried to reach a primary one.
- Numbers that conflict across sources — both numbers, both sources.
- Questions a strategy team will ask that this brief cannot answer.
- Anything whose freshness is doubtful, and why.

**Sources by tier** — counts only: Tier 1: N · Tier 2: N · Tier 3: N · Tier 4 (unverified): N
```
