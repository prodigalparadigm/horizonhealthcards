The gate caught its first card

Type 2 diabetes mellitus. Twenty-six claims checked, all seven cited FDA label PDFs opened rather than trusted.

What the card saidWhat the label actually says
Metformin, with a link to its FDA labelThat link resolves to a combination product of two drugs. A clinician clicking it lands on the wrong label.
Boxed warning lists renal impairment, sepsis, alcoholThe label also lists age 65 and over, and imaging with contrast. The contrast hold is a routine decision the card would not have prompted.
A kidney indication marked unverifiedIt is approved, and documented in the very label the card cites. The hedge became its own misinformation.

Verdict: BLOCK

Release is unavailable to the reviewer until these are corrected. Three defects, ten further fixes, on a card that read well.
