The headline is the whole argument: one request produces two different products, and neither reaches anyone before an independent check. Left card is the strategy brief: six sections, every claim sourced, a mandatory "could not confirm" section so the brief's edges are visible. Middle is the provider card: one page, ninety seconds, between patients. Boxed warnings are not optional - a card listing an agent without its warning is treated as defective. Right is the gate. The fact-checker opens every cited source rather than trusting the link, and it can block. Enforcement is structural, not procedural: staff accounts physically cannot write a card.
